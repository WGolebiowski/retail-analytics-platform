# Business KPI Framework

## Project Overview

The KPI framework supports analysis of sales performance, customer behavior, seller performance, product portfolio effectiveness, logistics costs, and customer segmentation.

Data source:
Olist E-Commerce Dataset

Analytical model:
Star Schema

Fact table:
- FACT_SALES

Dimensions:
- DIM_CUSTOMER
- DIM_PRODUCT
- DIM_SELLER
- DIM_DATE

---

# 1. Executive KPIs

## Revenue

Definition:
Total gross sales value generated by all sold order items.

Formula:

Revenue = SUM(sales_amount)

Business Purpose:
Measures overall sales performance.

---

## Number of Orders

Definition:
Total number of unique orders.

Formula:

Orders = COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)

Business Purpose:
Measures transaction volume.

---

## Number of Customers

Definition:
Total number of purchasing customers.

Formula:

Customers = COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)

Business Purpose:
Measures customer base size.

---

## Average Order Value (AOV)

Definition:
Average value generated per order.

Formula:

AOV = Revenue / Orders

Business Purpose:
Measures order profitability and customer spending behavior.

---

# 2. Sales Trend KPIs

## Monthly Revenue Trend

Definition:
Sales value aggregated by month.

Formula:

SUM(sales_amount)

Grouped By:
Year-Month

Business Purpose:
Identifies seasonality and sales trends.

---

## Month-over-Month Revenue Growth (MoM)

Definition:
Percentage change in product revenue compared to the previous month.

Formula:

(Current Month Revenue - Previous Month Revenue)
/
Previous Month Revenue

Business Purpose:
Measures short-term sales growth.

---

## Year-over-Year Revenue Growth (YoY)

Definition:
Percentage change in product revenue compared to the previous year.

Formula:

(Current Year Revenue - Previous Year Revenue)
/
Previous Year Revenue

Business Purpose:
Measures long-term growth.

---

# 3. Product Performance KPIs

## Revenue by Product Category

Definition:
Total sales value generated by product category.

Formula:

SUM(sales_amount)

Grouped By:
product_category_name_english

Business Purpose:
Identifies the most important product categories.

---

## Top Product Categories

Definition:
Top 10 categories ranked by revenue.

Business Purpose:
Highlights primary revenue drivers.

---

## Product Category Revenue Share

Definition:
Percentage contribution of a category to total revenue.

Formula:

Category Revenue
/
Total Revenue

Business Purpose:
Determines category importance.

---

## Product Category Pareto Analysis

Definition:
ABC classification based on cumulative revenue contribution.

Classes:

Category A
- First 80% of cumulative revenue

Category B
- Revenue contribution between 80% and 95%

Category C
- Remaining categories

Business Purpose:
Supports portfolio prioritization and category management.

---

## Top 10 Product Categories Contribution

Definition:
Percentage of revenue generated by the ten highest revenue categories.

Business Purpose:
Measures revenue concentration.

---

# 4. Customer KPIs

## Repeat Customer Rate

Definition:
Percentage of customers who placed more than one order.

Formula:

Repeat Customers
/
Total Customers

Business Purpose:
Measures customer retention.

---

## Customer Purchase Value

Definition:
Total historical value generated by a customer.

Metrics Included:

- Orders Count
- Product Revenue
- Freight Value
- Gross Sales Value
- Average Order Value

Business Purpose:
Identifies high-value customers.

---

# 5. Customer Segmentation KPIs

## RFM Segmentation

Definition:
Customer segmentation based on:

### Recency

Number of days since the customer's last purchase.

### Frequency

Number of completed orders.

### Monetary

Total product revenue generated by the customer.

Business Purpose:
Supports customer targeting and retention strategies.

---

## RFM Segments

Segments:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- New or Promising
- Regular Customers
- At Risk
- Inactive

Business Purpose:
Identifies different customer behavior patterns.

---

## RFM Segment Summary

Definition:
Aggregated performance metrics by customer segment.

Metrics:

- Number of Customers
- Product Revenue
- Average Customer Value
- Average Orders per Customer

Business Purpose:
Measures the business value of each customer segment.

---

# 6. Seller KPIs

## Top Sellers

Definition:
Top sellers ranked by generated revenue.

Formula:

SUM(sales_amount)

Business Purpose:
Identifies highest performing sellers.

---

## Seller Revenue Concentration

Definition:
Measures dependency on top sellers.

Metrics:

- Revenue Share %
- Cumulative Revenue %

Business Purpose:
Evaluates revenue concentration risk.
